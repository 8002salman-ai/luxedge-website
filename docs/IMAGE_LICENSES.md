# Luxedge image-source register

This register records the source of the editorial images currently referenced by the published guides. It is an internal provenance record, not a claim that every product-supplier image has been independently licensed by Luxedge.

## Editorial guide images

The seven guide hero images with a non-empty CMS URL are sourced from the following public image providers:

- Pexels images: `images.pexels.com` URLs in the CMS records for the bird-feeder, horse, cat-tunnel, and bird-cleaning guides. Review the current [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/) before reusing or replacing an image.
- Wikimedia Commons image: the cattle-trough guide uses the Wikimedia Commons image URL recorded in its CMS row. Preserve the image-page attribution and confirm the file's individual license on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) before republishing it in another context.

The no-pull harness guide currently has no hero image. Do not fill that gap with a random or AI-generated image presented as a real fitting demonstration.

## Product and site imagery

Product images are supplied through the catalog/media workflow and are not covered by this guide-image register. Before publishing a new product image, the operator must retain supplier/manufacturer permission or use an asset with a documented license. Product imagery must not be treated as evidence for dimensions, materials, certification, safety testing, or performance.

## Alt text

Hero image alt text is stored in the CMS `hero_image_alt` field. Alt text describes what is visibly present; it does not add keywords or claims that the image cannot establish.
